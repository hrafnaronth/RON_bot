RON-bot
Privacy Policy
Last updated: September 20, 2026
RON-bot is a Discord bot designed to provide game-related statistics, score tracking, season tracking and related features for communities playing The Ants: Underground Kingdom.

1. Information RON-bot may process

RON-bot may process and store information necessary to provide its features. This may include:

  • Discord user IDs
  • Discord usernames and display names
  • Discord server IDs
  • In-game player and profile names
  • Scores and personal-best records submitted to RON-bot
  • Season statistics and related game statistics
  • Information extracted from game screenshots
  • Timestamps associated with submissions and updates

RON-bot does not require users to provide passwords, payment-card information, home addresses or similar sensitive personal information.

2. Screenshot Processing

Users and server administrators may submit screenshots from The Ants: Underground Kingdom for automated statistics extraction.

RON-bot may use optical character recognition (OCR) to extract information such as an in-game player name, Soldier Defeats and Soldier Ants Healed.

Extracted statistics may be stored as part of RON-bot's statistics services. RON-bot does not need to permanently store the original screenshot after it has been processed.

3. How Information Is Used

Information processed by RON-bot may be used for:

  • Personal-best score tracking
  • Player profiles
  • Season statistics
  • Season calculations and comparisons
  • Estimated participation statistics
  • Spreadsheet reports
  • Displaying statistics within Discord

RON-bot does not sell personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

4. Data Storage and Service Providers

RON-bot may use third-party infrastructure and services necessary to provide its functionality, including:

  • Discord
  • MongoDB
  • Google Sheets and Google APIs
  • The hosting provider used to operate RON-bot

These services may process information as necessary to provide their respective functionality and may be subject to their own privacy policies.

5. Data Retention

Game statistics and related identifiers may be retained for as long as necessary to provide historical statistics, personal-best records, season records and other RON-bot functionality.

Information may be deleted when it is no longer required or following a valid deletion request, except where retention is reasonably required for legal, security or technical purposes.

6. Data Access, Correction and Deletion

Users may request access to applicable personal data associated with their Discord account and may request correction or deletion of that data.

RON-bot may require reasonable verification that the requester controls the relevant Discord account before processing such a request.

Deleting personal information may also remove associated profiles, scores, season records or other statistics where those records cannot reasonably be retained without identifying the user.

7. Administrator Submissions

Discord server administrators may submit game statistics for players who do not directly interact with RON-bot.

Such records may contain an in-game player name and game statistics without being linked to a Discord account.

8. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect information processed by RON-bot. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

RON-bot is intended for use through Discord and is not independently directed toward children below the minimum age permitted to use Discord.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as RON-bot gains or changes functionality. The date displayed at the top of this page indicates the latest revision.

11. Contact

Questions, access requests, correction requests or deletion requests concerning RON-bot data may be directed to the RON-bot developer through the official RON-bot support contact or Discord community.
