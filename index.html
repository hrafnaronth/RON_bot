RON-bot
The Ants: Underground Kingdom Discord Statistics Bot
RON-bot helps Discord communities track player records, personal best scores and season statistics for The Ants: Underground Kingdom.

Features

  • 🏆 Personal Best score tracking
  • 👤 Multiple player profiles
  • 📊 Season statistics
  • 📈 Player progress tracking
  • 📷 Statistics screenshot processing
  • ⚔️ Soldier Defeats tracking
  • ❤️ Soldier Ants Healed tracking
  • 📑 Season reports and statistics
